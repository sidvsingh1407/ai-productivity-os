# TarkaX Assessment Framework v1: Organizational Failure Intelligence

## Executive Summary

TarkaX is an **Organizational Failure Intelligence Platform**. The purpose of this framework is to establish a proprietary assessment methodology strictly designed for the detection, diagnosis, prediction, and prevention of organizational and operational failures.

This framework is **not** a traditional maturity model, nor a standard consulting survey structure. It is designed to evaluate organizational capability along a 5-stage scale (Fragile, Emerging, Operational, Scaled, Resilient) and map detected risks to a two-level failure taxonomy that directly incorporates standard APQC workflow ontology.

A successful assessment utilizing this framework will answer six critical questions for executive leadership:
1. **What is likely to fail?**
2. **Why will it fail?**
3. **How severe is the risk?**
4. **What capabilities are missing?**
5. **What should we do next?**
6. **What happens if we do nothing?**

## Part 1: Assessment Inventory

The TarkaX Assessment Portfolio provides an interconnected suite of diagnostic tools designed to capture signals of operational friction, misalignment, and impending failure at all levels of the organization.

### 1. AI Audit
* **Purpose:** Detect failures in the adoption, governance, and value realization of AI technologies.
* **Audience:** IT Leadership, AI Task Forces, Operations Directors.
* **Business Outcome:** Prevent shadow AI risk, eliminate redundant tooling spend, and identify immediate workflow bottlenecks solvable by standardized AI integration.
* **Estimated Time:** 15–20 minutes.
* **Data Collected:** AI tool inventory, usage frequency, risk management policies, value metrics.
* **Outputs Generated:** AI Adoption Risk Score, Shadow AI Footprint, Governance Capability Gap.

### 2. Workflow Diagnostic
* **Purpose:** Detect execution and handoff failures within specific operational domains.
* **Audience:** Process Owners, Functional Managers.
* **Business Outcome:** Identify invisible bottlenecks, remove manual friction, and prioritize automation/AI interventions.
* **Estimated Time:** 15–25 minutes per core workflow.
* **Data Collected:** Process steps (APQC mapped), cycle times, error rates, tool dependencies, handoff friction.
* **Outputs Generated:** Workflow Fragility Score, Bottleneck Map, Automation Potential.

### 3. Leadership Audit
* **Purpose:** Detect strategic misalignments and governance failures.
* **Audience:** C-Suite, VPs, Strategic Directors.
* **Business Outcome:** Ensure strategic directives translate into actionable execution reality without friction.
* **Estimated Time:** 10–15 minutes.
* **Data Collected:** Strategic priorities, perceived operational capability, investment allocation, risk appetite.
* **Outputs Generated:** Strategic Delusion Risk, Investment Misalignment Map.

### 4. Manager Audit
* **Purpose:** Detect execution, capacity, and reporting failures.
* **Audience:** Mid-level Managers, Team Leads, Department Heads.
* **Business Outcome:** Expose middle-management execution blockers and capacity constraints.
* **Estimated Time:** 15 minutes.
* **Data Collected:** Team capacity metrics, reporting overhead, escalation frequency, process adherence.
* **Outputs Generated:** Execution Fragility Risk, Capacity Burnout Forecast.

### 5. Employee Audit
* **Purpose:** Detect tool friction, process ambiguity, and cultural resistance at the edge.
* **Audience:** Individual Contributors, Frontline Staff.
* **Business Outcome:** Quantify the "tax" of poor processes and bad tooling on daily work.
* **Estimated Time:** 10 minutes.
* **Data Collected:** Tool frustration, task clarity, manual rework time, knowledge access difficulty.
* **Outputs Generated:** Friction Tax Calculation, Cultural Resistance Risk.

### 6. Organizational Alignment Audit
* **Purpose:** Detect perceptual gaps and structural contradictions between hierarchical tiers.
* **Audience:** Executive Leadership (Output consumer).
* **Business Outcome:** Quantify the "Reality Gap" between what leadership believes and what employees experience.
* **Estimated Time:** N/A (Derived Assessment).
* **Data Collected:** Synthesized outputs from Leadership, Manager, and Employee Audits.
* **Outputs Generated:** Alignment Gap Analysis, Contradiction Report.

## Part 2: Assessment Dimensions

Assessments do not measure arbitrary traits; they measure dimensions that act as early warning signals for systemic failure.

### AI Audit Dimensions
* **Visibility:** Do we know where and how AI is being used? (Detects: Shadow AI Failure)
* **Governance:** Do we have guardrails to prevent catastrophic misuse? (Detects: Compliance/Security Failure)
* **Value Extraction:** Is AI actually accelerating throughput, or just adding complexity? (Detects: ROI Failure)
* **Dependency:** How vulnerable are core operations to external AI vendor outages? (Detects: Technology Failure)

### Workflow Diagnostic Dimensions
* **Friction & Bottlenecks:** Where does work wait or break? (Detects: Execution Failure)
* **Standardization:** Is work performed consistently across the team? (Detects: Knowledge Failure)
* **Handoff Quality:** Do downstream processes receive what they need? (Detects: Communication Failure)
* **Tooling Efficacy:** Do tools enable the work or act as a barrier? (Detects: Technology Failure)

### Leadership Audit Dimensions
* **Strategic Reality:** Is the strategy grounded in operational capacity? (Detects: Leadership Failure)
* **Capital Allocation:** Are resources flowing to the actual bottlenecks? (Detects: Execution Failure)
* **Change Readiness Perception:** Does leadership accurately assess organizational fatigue? (Detects: Change Failure)

### Manager Audit Dimensions
* **Capacity Reality:** Is the team chronically over-utilized to mask process inefficiencies? (Detects: Execution/Workflow Failure)
* **Escalation Velocity:** How often do routine issues require managerial intervention? (Detects: Knowledge/Governance Failure)
* **Reporting Overhead:** How much time is wasted feeding metrics upward rather than executing? (Detects: Workflow Failure)

### Employee Audit Dimensions
* **Process Clarity:** Do employees understand *how* to do the work, or just *what* the goal is? (Detects: Knowledge Failure)
* **Tool Friction:** How many workarounds are required to complete a standard task? (Detects: Technology Failure)
* **Cognitive Load:** How difficult is it to find the information required to execute? (Detects: Knowledge Silo Failure)

## Part 3: Question Framework

The goal is not to write questionnaires, but to design a framework that extracts evidence of impending failure. Every question must tie back to a capability level (Fragile, Emerging, Operational, Scaled, Resilient).

### Dimension Example: Workflow Handoff Quality
* **What is measured:** The structural integrity of work passing between teams or stages.
* **Why it matters:** Handoffs are the most frequent site of Execution Failure and Communication Failure. Poor handoffs cause systemic rework.
* **Example Question Types:**
  * *Frequency of Rework:* "What percentage of inputs received from Team A require manual correction before you can begin your task?" (Objective metric)
  * *Standardization:* "Is the format of the incoming deliverable strictly enforced by a system, or does it vary?" (Systemic evaluation)

### Dimension Example: AI Visibility
* **What is measured:** The degree to which the organization tracks decentralized AI usage.
* **Why it matters:** Shadow AI introduces massive Compliance Failure risk and prevents scaling best practices.
* **Example Question Types:**
  * *Discovery Mechanism:* "How are new AI tools discovered and approved for team use?" (Process evaluation)
  * *Vendor Sprawl:* "How many unique AI subscriptions are expensed outside of central IT?" (Financial/Risk indicator)

### Dimension Example: Strategic Reality (Leadership)
* **What is measured:** The gap between strategic goals and actual operational capacity.
* **Why it matters:** Leadership Failure occurs when mandates exceed structural capabilities, causing burnout and execution collapse.
* **Example Question Types:**
  * *Capacity Awareness:* "When prioritizing new initiatives, how do you verify the organization has the slack capacity to execute them?" (Governance evaluation)

## Part 4: Evidence Requirements

Evidence must transition smoothly from Self-Reported (Current State) to System-Integrated (Future State) without breaking the core Failure Intelligence engine.

### Leadership Evidence
* **Self-Reported:** Budget allocations, strategic OKR documents, stated risk tolerance profiles.
* **System Integrations:** Financial/ERP systems (budget vs. actuals), OKR tracking software (goal progression vs. stagnation).

### Manager Evidence
* **Self-Reported:** Estimated team utilization, primary escalation categories, list of manual reporting tasks.
* **System Integrations:** Jira/Asana velocity metrics, HRMS overtime data, Slack/Teams channel communication density (e.g., high activity outside core hours indicating friction).

### Employee Evidence
* **Self-Reported:** Workaround frequency, estimated time spent searching for information, perceived tool utility.
* **System Integrations:** SSO/IdP login frequencies (tool sprawl), internal wiki search failure rates, helpdesk ticket volumes for routine access.

### Workflow Evidence
* **Self-Reported:** Process descriptions (APQC mappings), process owners, error frequency estimates, required manual steps.
* **System Integrations:** Process mining logs, CRM state-change timestamps (measuring actual cycle time), ERP transaction logs.

### AI Evidence
* **Self-Reported:** Tools used, perceived time saved, data sensitivity involved in AI prompts.
* **System Integrations:** Network traffic analysis (identifying shadow AI domains), expense management systems (identifying unauthorized AI subscriptions).

## Part 5: Output Framework

TarkaX does not deliver static survey scores. It delivers **Failure Intelligence**. The outputs generated across all assessments strictly map to the CEO's six critical questions.

### Universal Outputs
Every assessment (regardless of level) must generate:
1. **Current Capability State:** (Fragile, Emerging, Operational, Scaled, Resilient).
2. **Target Capability State:** The required state based on business context.
3. **Primary Failure Risks:** Ranked by severity and likelihood.
4. **Missing Capabilities:** The structural deficits causing the risk.

### Specific Assessment Outputs
* **AI Audit:** Shadow AI Risk Matrix, Governance Gap Report, Vendor Redundancy Map.
* **Workflow Diagnostic:** Cycle Time Forecast, Automation ROI Projection, Bottleneck Isolation Map.
* **Leadership Audit:** Strategic Delusion Index (misalignment of goals vs. capacity).
* **Manager/Employee Audit:** Execution Tax Calculation (hours lost to friction), Burnout/Turnover Risk Forecast.

## Part 6: Alignment Audit Design

The Alignment Audit acts as the **Contradiction Engine**. It does not collect its own data; it synthesizes the Leadership, Manager, and Employee Audits to detect structural contradictions.

### Comparison Framework
* **Vertical Alignment:** Does Leadership’s perception of capability match the Employee’s reality?
* **Horizontal Alignment:** Do distinct business units (e.g., Sales vs. Delivery) possess fundamentally different capability levels for the same APQC process?

### Dimension Comparison Examples
* **Capability Perception Gap:**
  * *Leadership:* "We have Scaled AI adoption."
  * *Employees:* "We have Fragile AI adoption (high friction, no training)."
  * *Contradiction Engine Output:* "High Risk: Strategic Delusion. Leadership expects productivity gains that operational reality cannot support."
* **Friction Perception Gap:**
  * *Managers:* "Process execution is smooth."
  * *Employees:* "Process requires 3 hours of manual workarounds daily."
  * *Contradiction Engine Output:* "High Risk: Hidden Execution Tax. Management is blind to structural inefficiencies masking as employee effort."

### Alignment Scoring
Gaps are classified by severity rather than percentage:
* **Congruent:** Perceptions match reality (Low Risk).
* **Minor Gap:** Perceptions differ by one capability level (Watch List).
* **Critical Contradiction:** Perceptions differ by two or more capability levels (High Failure Risk).

## Part 7: Failure Intelligence Taxonomy

To enable TarkaX to detect, diagnose, predict, and prevent failures, all risks must map to a standardized two-level taxonomy.

### LEVEL 1: Strategic Failure Categories
These are the executive-level classifications of why an organization fails to execute.
* **Governance Failure:** Lack of oversight, controls, or compliance mechanisms.
* **Execution Failure:** Inability to deliver outputs at the required speed or quality.
* **Communication Failure:** Breakdown in handoffs, siloed data, or misaligned priorities.
* **Knowledge Failure:** Inability to locate, share, or retain critical operational information.
* **Change Failure:** Inability to absorb new processes, tools, or structures without severe disruption.
* **Technology Failure:** Tool sprawl, system outages, or systems acting as friction rather than enablers.
* **AI Adoption Failure:** Shadow usage, failed ROI, or algorithmic risk.
* **Leadership Failure:** Disconnect between strategic mandates and operational reality.

### LEVEL 2: APQC Mapping (The Operational Context)
Every Level 1 Failure is explicitly mapped to the standard APQC framework to pinpoint *where* the failure is occurring.

*Example Failure Chains:*
* `Execution Failure` → `APQC: Deliver Physical Products (4.0)` → `Manage Logistics and Warehousing (4.4)`
* `Knowledge Failure` → `APQC: Manage Human Capital (7.0)` → `Develop and Train Employees (7.3)`
* `AI Adoption Failure` → `APQC: Manage Financial Resources (8.0)` → `Process Accounts Payable (8.3)`

This taxonomy ensures that TarkaX provides the CEO with the **Strategic view** ("We have a systemic Knowledge Failure") and the Process Owner with the **Operational view** ("We need to fix training in Accounts Payable").

## Part 8: Assessment Relationships

The TarkaX framework is modular but highly interconnected. Assessments can run standalone, but their value compound when integrated.

* **Core Hub:** The **Workflow Diagnostic** is the operational anchor. All failure intelligence ultimately impacts workflows.
* **Specialized Probes:** The **AI Audit** is a specialized, standalone probe that feeds technology and governance failure data into the Workflow Diagnostic.
* **Hierarchical Stack:** The **Leadership**, **Manager**, and **Employee Audits** form a vertical stack. They are best run concurrently to feed the **Alignment Audit** (The Contradiction Engine).
* **The Master Engine:** The **Alignment Audit** sits above the others, consuming their outputs to generate the ultimate Organizational Failure Intelligence Report.

*Visual Relationship Mapping:*
```
[ Leadership Audit ] --\
[ Manager Audit ]    -----> [ Alignment Audit (Contradiction Engine) ] ---> [ Executive Failure Report ]
[ Employee Audit ]   --/

[ AI Audit ] -------------> [ Workflow Diagnostic ] ----------------------> [ Operational Improvement Plan ]
```

## Conclusion: Meeting the Success Criteria

This proprietary framework ensures that regardless of which specific assessment is deployed, the final artifact answers the CEO's critical questions:

1. **What is likely to fail?** (Identified via the Level 2 APQC mapping).
2. **Why will it fail?** (Identified via the Level 1 Strategic Failure category and specific Capability Deficit).
3. **How severe is the risk?** (Scored via the Contradiction Engine and Capability Gap magnitude).
4. **What capabilities are missing?** (Exposed by the Fragile -> Resilient delta).
5. **What should we do next?** (Tied directly to moving from the Current State to Target State capability tier).
6. **What happens if we do nothing?** (Calculated as the Execution Tax, Shadow AI Risk, or Turnover Forecast in the Output Framework).

This architecture sets the foundation for TarkaX to act as a system of record for organizational truth, not just a surveying tool.
